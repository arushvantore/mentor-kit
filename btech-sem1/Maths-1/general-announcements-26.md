Hi @channel
I know many of you have come to Bangalore from different states, and the weather and climate here may be different from what you are used to. Due to the change in climate and the ongoing monsoon season, some of you may be feeling unwell.

As you know, it is currently the monsoon season, and we are experiencing frequent rainfall. This can also lead to an increase in mosquitoes and the risk of mosquito-borne illnesses such as dengue and malaria. Therefore, please take the following precautions.
• Please use mosquito repellent such as Odomos, especially during the evening.
• Try to wear full-sleeve clothes and trousers, particularly in the evening.
• Please keep your windows closed when you leave for college and during the evening/night to reduce the chances of mosquitoes entering your rooms.
• Drink safe/boiled water and stay well hydrated.
• Maintain proper personal hygiene and keep your rooms clean.
• Please avoid keeping stagnant water in or around your rooms.
• If you experience fever, body pain, severe headache, vomiting, weakness, or any other unusual symptoms, please consult a doctor at the earliest.
From our side, we are also taking preventive measures. Mosquito fogging and pest control will be carried out tomorrow for both hostels to help control mosquitoes and maintain a safe environment.
I kindly request everyone to take these precautions seriously and take good care of yourselves and your roommates. Stay safe, and don’t hesitate to seek medical help if you are unwell. 
If you are feeling unwell, please inform us or your wardens immediately so that we can support you and take the necessary action.
Stay safe and take care of your health!

Sharing two podcasts I'd recommend listening this week. Both point to a shift that is shaping how products will be build over the next few years:

Radical change in interface
We're moving from apps and chat boxes to agents that live inside the channels you already use (text, email, phone, Slack) and simply get things done.

The edge is no longer the model
The breakout products of 2026 aren't just training better models. They're taking capabilities that already exist and wrapping them around real human problems and human interactions: booking, buying, negotiating, coordinating. Instinct's annualized gross transaction volume has passed roughly $1 billion.


 GrokBot → youtube.com/watch?v=maSdsTLaMuU
An agent with its own cloud computer that logs into your tools and works around the clock. Notice how much of the excitement is about speed, connectivity and out-of-the-box experience, not just raw intelligence.


 Instinct → music.youtube.com/watch?v=…
A 23-year-old founder building an AI assistant with no app at all. Listen for how he thinks about making the product predictable before making it powerful, and why staying inside people's existing communication channels beat building a new one.

As you listen, think about:

What human problem is each product actually solving? Where is the AI, and where is the product design?
Why does "no new interface" work better than a shiny new app here?
Pick one everyday problem on campus. How would you solve it with today's models, without needing a better one? How will you make interaction highly organic and intuitive?
