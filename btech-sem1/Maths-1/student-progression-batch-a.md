@channel Hi Students,
We’re opening nominations for Class Committee – Subject Representatives 
The Subject Representative will help build a strong connection between students and faculty for a particular subject.
As a Subject Representative, you’ll get an opportunity to:
• Work closely with faculty and the Progression Team
• Understand the academic needs and challenges of your batch
• Bring student perspectives and feedback to faculty, and communicate relevant updates and action items back to students.
• Take initiative to improve the learning experience for your batch
• Build your communication, leadership and problem-solving skills
• Work collaboratively with students and faculty to find solutions
I’m also sharing a document so you can understand the role, selection criteria and process, and expectations better: docs.google.com/document/d/1gybwN-xF3JA…/edit?tab=t.0
The role is not completely set in stone. Once the representatives are selected, we’ll work together to think about what else this role can look like and how we can make it more meaningful for students and faculty. So if you have ideas for how Subject Representatives can contribute beyond what’s mentioned in the document, there will be space to bring those ideas in.
If you enjoy taking initiative , working with people and want to contribute to making your learning experience better , consider nominating yourself.
 docs.google.com/forms/…/viewform?usp=dialog
