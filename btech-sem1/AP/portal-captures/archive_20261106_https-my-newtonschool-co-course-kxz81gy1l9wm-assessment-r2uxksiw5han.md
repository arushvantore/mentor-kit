Substituting the values: (4 × 6) + (2 × 0) = (4 + 2)v. Therefore, 24 = 6v, giving the common velocity v = 4 m/s.

A
3 m/s
B
4 m/s
6 m/s
Explanation:According to the work-energy theorem, the net work done is equal to the change in kinetic energy. The formula is W = ½mv² − ½mu², where W is work done, m is mass, v is final velocity, and u is initial velocity. Since the object starts from rest, u = 0. Substituting the values: 36 = ½ × 2 × v², so v² = 36. Therefore, the final speed is 6 m/s.

Explanation:The pseudo force acting on an object in an accelerating reference frame is given by the formula Fₚ = ma, where m is the mass of the object and a is the acceleration of the reference frame.
